Results of the spring session of the bar examination 2026

The spring session of the bar examination was held in the Hall of Justice in March and April 2026. Fifty-four candidates sat the examination; twenty-two passed.

On 24 April 2026 the new members of the bar took the oath before Chief Justice Ayyub Nazarbekov and were entered on the roll. In his address the Chief Justice reminded them that the lawyer serves the court as well as the client.

Press service of the Supreme Court
