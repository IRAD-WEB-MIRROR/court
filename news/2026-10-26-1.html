Accreditation of journalists to the sitting of the Talas District Court on 16 November 2026

The Talas District Court will continue the hearing of case TDC-C 1307/2026, Talas Commercial Development Group v. Liberty Forum, on 16 November 2026. The interim injunction was refused on 12 October 2026 and the case adjourned.

Journalists who wish to attend apply to the press service by 12 November 2026 at press@court.iradgov.org, giving the name of the outlet and a copy of the press card. The number of seats in the courtroom is limited. Photography and filming are allowed only before the sitting opens.

The press service does not comment on pending cases.
