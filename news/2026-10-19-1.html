19 October 2026 · No. 15

Results of the autumn session of the bar examination 2026

The autumn session of the bar examination was held in the Hall of Justice in September and October 2026. Sixty-one candidates sat the examination; twenty-seven passed.

The oath will be administered by the Chief Justice on 30 October 2026. With the new members the roll of the bar will count about 1,180 advocates.

Applications for the spring session 2027 are received by the Registry until 31 January 2027. See Admission to the bar.
