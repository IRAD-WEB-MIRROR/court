Training of court staff on the keeping of case registers

On 13–17 October 2025 the Bar Admissions and Training Office held a course for clerks of the Supreme Court, the district courts and the magistrate courts on the keeping of case registers and the numbering of cases.

Forty-one clerks took part. The course prepares the courts for the publication of a register of pending cases, as foreseen in the plan of work of the Court for 2026.

Press service of the Supreme Court
