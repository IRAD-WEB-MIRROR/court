20 December 2019 · No. 22

Restoration of the Hall of Justice completed

The restoration works at the Hall of Justice begun in April 2018 are completed. The roof, the granite facade and the main steps are repaired, and the windows of the main courtroom are replaced.

From 23 December 2019 visitors again enter by the main steps. The reading room of the Law Library reopens on the same day; hours of the reading room: Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00.
