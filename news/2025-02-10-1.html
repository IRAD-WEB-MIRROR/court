Annual report on the work of the courts for 2024 published

The Supreme Court published the annual report on the work of the courts of the Zone for 2024.

In 2024 the district courts decided 17,210 cases and the magistrate courts of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl about 40,200 matters. The Supreme Court received 59 appeals, petitions and applications and decided 55.

The report is available in the section Documents and in print in the Law Library.
