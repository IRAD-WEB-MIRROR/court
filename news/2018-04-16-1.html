Restoration works at the Hall of Justice

From 16 April 2018 restoration works are carried out at the Hall of Justice: repair of the roof, cleaning and repair of the granite facade and of the main steps. The works are planned to the end of 2019.

Sittings of the Court continue as listed. During the works visitors enter the building through the side entrance from the courtyard gate. The reading room of the Law Library is closed until further notice.

Press service of the Supreme Court
