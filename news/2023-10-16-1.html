Results of the autumn session of the bar examination 2023

The autumn session of the bar examination was held in the Hall of Justice on 25–26 September (written papers) and 9–11 October 2023 (oral examination).

Forty-eight candidates sat the examination; nineteen passed. The oath will be administered by the Chief Justice on 27 October 2023, after which the new members are entered on the roll of the bar.

Candidates who did not pass may apply again for a later session under the Rules of the Bar Examination.

Press service of the Supreme Court
