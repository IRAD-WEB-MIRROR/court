Open day for students of law

On 11 April 2025 the Supreme Court held an open day for students of the Faculty of Law of Talas State University. About ninety students of the third and fourth year visited the Hall of Justice.

The students attended a public sitting of the Court, visited the Registry, the Law Library and the Interpretation Service, and were told about the bar examination and about work in the courts. The Registrar answered questions on internships in the Registry.

The open day is held each spring with the Faculty of Law.
