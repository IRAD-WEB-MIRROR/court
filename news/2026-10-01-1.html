Register of pending cases opened online

From 1 October 2026 the register of cases pending before the Supreme Court and the Talas District Court is published on this site and updated each week. The register is also listed among the services of the e-Irad public services portal.

For each case the register shows its number, the court, the date of filing, the title and kind of the case, the next sitting and the status. Parties who are private persons are shown by initials. The register follows Registry Instruction No. 4 of 21 September 2026.
