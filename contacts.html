Contacts and reception

Address, telephones, hours of the Registry and the reception of citizens. Information on a pending case is given by the Registry to the parties and their counsel; the public part of it is in the register of pending cases.

Written appeals

Written appeals of citizens are sent by post to the address above, by e-mail to registry@court.iradgov.org, or handed in at the Registry counter. You may use the e-Irad form OG-1 (appeal of a citizen to a state body). Give your full name, postal address and, where it concerns a case, the case number. The reply is sent within 30 days of registration. Anonymous appeals are not considered. The Court does not give legal advice and does not answer, outside the proceedings, questions on the merits of a pending case; complaints against a judgment are made only by appeal in the procedure set by law.

How to get here

The Hall of Justice stands on Liberty Boulevard in the centre of Talas. City buses and marshrutkas along Liberty Boulevard stop at "Adilet Üyü". From Talas railway station and the Central Bus Station take any marshrutka to the centre and walk along the boulevard. Parking on the boulevard is limited; the courtyard is for staff and prison transport only. Entrance for visitors is by the main steps; visitors with limited mobility use the side entrance from the courtyard gate, after calling the duty officer. Please bring your passport or identity card; bags are checked at the entrance.
