2025

  • 16 December 2025 · No. 24
    Sittings during the New Year holidays

    By Order of the Chief Justice No. 37-o of 15 December 2025 the Supreme Court does not sit from 29 December 2025 to 9 January 2026. The first sitting after the holidays is on 14 January 2026.

2019

  • 20 December 2019 · No. 22
    Restoration of the Hall of Justice completed

  • 25 November 2019 · No. 18
    New Rules of the Bar Examination approved

    On 22 November 2019 the Plenum of the Supreme Court by Resolution No. 4 approved the Rules of the Bar Examination. The Rules replace the earlier procedure and apply from the spring session 2020.

2018

  • 16 April 2018 · No. 7

    From 16 April 2018 restoration works are carried out at the Hall of Justice: repair of the roof, cleaning and repair of the granite facade and of the main steps. The works are planned to the end of 2019.
