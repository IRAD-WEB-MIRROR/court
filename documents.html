Resolutions of the Plenum of the Supreme Court, the Rules of Court, instructions of the Registry, orders of the Chief Justice and the annual reports on the work of the courts. Laws of the Zone are published in the Official Gazette. The texts here are for information; in case of difference the text kept in the Registry prevails.

Annual report · 6 February 2026 · AR-2025

Report on the work of the courts of the Zone in 2025

Published

District courts: 17,940 cases decided (civil 7,310; commercial 2,460; criminal 6,020, of them 38 with a jury; administrative 2,150). Magistrate courts: about 41,600 matters. Supreme Court: 63 cases received (44 appeals, 12 commercial, 4 petitions for constitutional review, 3 matters of the bar), 58 decided, 21 pending at the end of the year; average time 5.1 months.

Order of the Chief Justice · 15 December 2025 · No. 37-o

On the sittings of the Supreme Court during the New Year holidays 2025–2026

Executed

No sittings from 29 December 2025 to 9 January 2026; first sitting 14 January 2026; the Registry works on working days; urgent applications through the duty officer to the duty justice.

Annual report · 7 February 2025 · AR-2024

Report on the work of the courts of the Zone in 2024

Published

District courts: 17,210 cases decided. Magistrate courts of Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl: about 40,200 matters. Supreme Court: 59 cases received, 55 decided. Staff of the courts, training, interpreters and the restoration of the court buildings.

Instruction of the Registry · 28 February 2024 · No. 2

In force

Rules of Court · 21 January 2008 · No. 1

Rules of Court of the Supreme Court of Irad

In force, as amended in 2019 and 2026

Procedure before the Supreme Court: filing of appeals and petitions (Part II), languages of the proceedings and interpreters (Rule 12), the answer of the Government to a petition for constitutional review (Rule 41), listing of cases for hearing by order of the Chief Justice (Rule 44), public sittings, panels and the Plenum, the Registry and the cause lists.
