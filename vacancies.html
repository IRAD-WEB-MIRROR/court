Vacancies

Vacant posts in the Registry and the services of the Hall of Justice. Appointments are made after a competition held by the Personnel Department. Posts of justices and judges are not filled through this page.

Open competitions

SC-26/14

Registry clerk (2 posts)

Place of work
Registry, Hall of Justice, Talas
Grade
Court service, grade 5; from 2,950 som a month
Applications until
20 November 2026

Duties: receiving and registering appeals, petitions and other filings at the counter; entering cases in the register and keeping it up to date; preparing cause lists and notices to parties; issuing certified copies of judgments; answering enquiries of the public by telephone and at the counter.

Requirements: higher education in law (a final-year student of law may be considered); good written Iradi and English, Russian an advantage; accurate work with documents and a computer; politeness with the public.

SC-26/15

Court interpreter (Iradi, Russian, English)

Place of work
Interpretation Service, Hall of Justice, Talas; sittings of the district courts as required
Grade
Court service, grade 4; from 3,400 som a month
Applications until
27 November 2026

Duties: oral interpretation at hearings of the Supreme Court and, on assignment, of the district and magistrate courts in Talas and Nurzhan; written translation of judgments, procedural documents and evidence between Iradi, Russian and English.

Requirements: higher education in languages or in law; fluent Iradi and Russian and good English, written and spoken; experience of consecutive interpretation; willingness to travel to Nurzhan. Candidates take a written and an oral test.

SC-26/16

IT specialist (case register and website)

Place of work
Registry, Hall of Justice, Talas
Grade
Court service, grade 4; from 3,900 som a month
Applications until
4 December 2026

Duties: support of the case register and its weekly publication on the site of the Court; maintenance of the site in three languages; administration of the computers, printers and network of the Hall of Justice; help to the staff; backups of the Registry data.

Requirements: higher technical education (Nurzhan Polytechnic Institute, Talas State University or equivalent); two years of experience; knowledge of networks and of web publishing; working English and Iradi.

SC-26/17

Court bailiff (3 posts)

Place of work
Court Bailiffs and Security Service, Hall of Justice, Talas
Grade
Court service, grade 6; from 2,800 som a month, with uniform
Applications until
15 December 2026

Duties: keeping order in the courtrooms and the Hall of Justice; checking visitors and bags at the entrance; escorting detained persons to and from hearings together with the prison escort; delivering summonses; night and holiday duty by roster.

Requirements: secondary education or higher; age 21 to 45; good health and physical fitness; no criminal record; service in the State Defense Corps, the Gendarmerie or the police an advantage; Iradi and Russian spoken.

How to apply

Send an application quoting the reference of the post, a curriculum vitae, copies of the diploma and the passport, and a recent photograph to the Personnel Department by e-mail to personnel@court.iradgov.org or by hand to room 112 of the Hall of Justice (Monday to Friday, 09:00–13:00 and 14:00–17:00).

Applications are received until 17:00 Irad Time on the closing date. Candidates on the short list are invited to a written test and an interview with a commission of the Court; others are not contacted. The appointment is made by order of the Chief Justice.

Enquiries: Personnel Department, Nurzat Sarbagysheva, telephone +996-39-2-62-10-30.
