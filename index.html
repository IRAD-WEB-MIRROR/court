Supreme Court of Irad

The Supreme Court is the highest court of the Irad Special Economic Zone. Established in November 1992 under the Organic Act, it hears final appeals, reviews legislation and executive acts for conformity with the basic law, and regulates the legal profession. It sits in the Hall of Justice on Liberty Boulevard in Talas.
